# Research: Fleet page vs. design mockup — where the layout differs

**Date**: 2026-09-06T13:23:35+02:00
**Researcher**: Claude (Fable 5.1)
**Git Commit**: 074cb5f9a564987bbf21b97667c4017792f87088
**Branch**: worktree-fix-fleet-page-layout-parity
**Repository**: car-rental

## Research Question

Check the layout inconsistencies between the design and the app on the fleet page, especially the calendar layout and the information about the car. Present the differences so the owner can decide what goes into planning.

## Method

- Pulled the live design source with DesignSync: `customer-desktop.jsx` (fleet browse, desktop / tablet / mobile), `customer-desktop-reserve.jsx` (desktop vehicle detail + booking widget), `customer-screens.jsx` (mobile vehicle detail), `shared.jsx` (tokens, `DayCell`, `busyHalves`, Polish strings).
- Rendered the design boards myself from that source with the app's own variable Inter font (harness in the session scratchpad; the boards are `d-fleet`, `t-fleet`, `m-fleet`, `d-detail`). The canonical desktop detail render is saved as `design-review/d-detail-mock.png`.
- Captured the running app (commit `074cb5f`, Polish locale) at 1440 / 834 / 390 with Playwright, then measured the desktop detail page with `getComputedStyle` and `getBoundingClientRect`, before and after selecting a date range. The selected-range widget capture is `design-review/app-d-detail-widget-selected.png`.
- Two sub-agents produced an exact-values inventory of the app code and the historical decision record (archived plans, design contracts, impl-reviews).

## Summary

**The `/fleet` listing matches its design.** It closed a rendered vision-diff gate in the `landing-fleet-restyle` change and the residual differences are all recorded, accepted deviations. One new drift exists on mobile: the design's type pills now wrap into rows, while the app still ships the dark horizontal scroller the design had on 2026-08-02.

**The vehicle detail page (`/fleet/<id>`) is where the inconsistencies are.** It was built in June against an early export, was explicitly left out of the August restyle ("deferred to a later change"), and has **no design contract at all**. The design source for it exists (`ScreenDesktopDetail` in `customer-desktop-reserve.jsx`), which contradicts `design-system.md`'s claim that the S-02 detail screens are "screenshot-only, no recoverable JSX". The repo's stored mockup `desktop-1-vehicle-detail-dates.png` is a stale export (older legend, struck-out busy days, a green status pill the live design no longer has).

The differences fall into three groups:

1. **Vehicle information (desktop).** The design puts the eyebrow, the 48px title and a model subtitle **left-aligned outside any card**, then a separate white image card. The app centers the eyebrow and title **inside** the gallery card and has no subtitle. Spec tiles in the design are compact (icon and label on one row, value below, 18px padding); the app stacks a 32px icon box above the label, making tiles ~50% taller. The design's "what's included" row has no cards and three distinct green icons; the app renders three white cards with the same check icon.
2. **Calendar (desktop widget).** The design frames the month in a bordered, rounded box with a left-aligned "Marzec 2026" caption and two small square nav buttons on the right, 2-letter weekday headers, 32px cells with a 4px gap, no outside-month days, a permanent 3-item legend, and half-cell diagonals with a divider line for changeover days. The app renders a bare grid with a centered uppercase caption, ghost nav buttons at the far edges, 3-letter lowercase weekdays, ~43px cells with an 8px gap, outside-month days shown, a "today" highlight the design does not have, no legend unless busy days exist, and a lighter half-cell treatment with no divider (known open item D14).
3. **Widget chrome and copy.** Radius 22 vs 16, price 30px vs 24px, missing "Odbiór i zwrot" label, date fields with a 20px radius instead of 12px and no calendar icon, CTA "Zarezerwuj" vs "Zarezerwuj ten pojazd", "/doba" vs "/dzień", "Szacunkowa cena" vs "Szac. koszt", and a different reassurance sentence.

Three design elements are **deliberately absent** in the app and should not be re-flagged without a decision: the green "Dostępny w Twoim terminie" strip (D4), the plate in the eyebrow (D6, no schema), and the mobile flow where the detail page has no calendar and a "Sprawdź dostępność" CTA leads to a separate reservation screen (S-02 chose "Option A": the detail page hosts the booking widget).

### 8. The calendar width cap (owner-reported symptom), confirmed and quantified

The most visible symptom on the detail page is that the calendar sits inset, with empty space to its left and right, while the design's calendar runs edge to edge inside the widget. Measured on both sides at 1440:

- **App**: widget inner content width 470.41px; the calendar is wrapped in `mx-auto mt-3 w-full max-w-[300px]` (`BookingWidget.tsx:200`), so it renders 300px wide with a computed margin of `12px 85.2031px 0px`. That is **85.2px of empty space on each side**.
- **Design**: widget inner content width 436.88px; the bordered calendar frame is 436.88px, flush to both inner edges. **Side gap 0.**
- Every other element in the app's widget spans the full inner width: the Odbiór/Zwrot field row is 470.41px and the CTA is 470.41px. The calendar is the only inset child, which is what makes it read as misaligned rather than merely narrow.

The cap is deliberate and carries a source comment (`BookingWidget.tsx:196-199`): "Capped to a centered max-width so the desktop column can't stretch it into loose wide cells". The design resolves that same tension differently: it lets the grid fill and makes the cells **wide rectangles** (54.13 × 32px, ratio ≈ 1.7:1) instead of squares.

**This is why it is not a one-line fix.** The shared `ui/calendar.tsx` forces square cells: `classNames.day` carries `aspect-square` and the day button carries `aspect-square size-auto w-full min-w-(--cell-size)` (`calendar.tsx:104-112, 186-193`). Removing only the 300px cap would widen each column to ~62.5px and, because of `aspect-square`, make every row 62.5px tall, growing the calendar from 273px to roughly 430px tall. That is exactly the "loose" result the comment guards against. Matching the design requires removing the cap **and** overriding the square aspect with an explicit cell height, either locally from `BookingWidget` or in the shared component.

### 9. The legend is implemented but invisible on all current local data

The design's legend is unconditional. The app's is wrapped in `availability.size > 0` (`BookingWidget.tsx:268`), which is only true when `get_vehicle_busy_ranges` returns a row. That RPC floors at `return_date >= current_date` (`supabase/migrations/20260616120000_vehicle_busy_ranges_date_floor.sql:26`).

Every booking in the local database has expired:

| Today      | Reservations | Latest `return_date` |
| ---------- | ------------ | -------------------- |
| 2026-09-06 | 8            | 2026-09-05           |

So the RPC returns nothing for every vehicle, no day gets a `blocked` / `pickupOnly` / `returnOnly` modifier, and **the legend renders on no vehicle at all**. The seed writes several rows as `current_date - 4, current_date`, which Postgres evaluates once at insert time, so they age out as the machine clock advances past the last seed run.

Verified by temporarily inserting two future bookings on the Sprinter (2026-09-11 → 09-13 and 09-18 → 09-20), capturing the widget, then deleting them and confirming the table returned to 8 rows with the same latest return date. With those rows present the legend rendered correctly with all three items and the half-cell diagonals appeared on the changeover days. Capture: `design-review/app-d-detail-widget-legend.png`.

Two consequences for planning:

- A missing legend on a local run is a **data artefact, not a layout defect**. Any vision-diff of this surface must seed a future booking first or it compares against a state the design never draws.
- The half-cell treatment is only ever visible under that same condition, so the D14 fill reconciliation cannot be reviewed on default seed data either.

Content differences, independent of visibility: the design explains the **selected** colour with a `Wybrane` swatch, which the app has no equivalent for; the design uses one combined half-day item with explanatory copy (`Dzień odbioru / zwrotu — wciąż dostępny`), the app uses two terse directional items (`tylko odbiór`, `tylko zwrot`); the design carries a hairline top border and spans the calendar frame, the app has no border and inherits the same 300px cap as the calendar.

### 10. The "what's included" row below the specs (owner-reported), measured

This is the third row of car information under the photo, after the two rows of spec tiles. Measured on both sides at their own canonical widths. Crops: `design-review/crop-design-trustrow.png`, `design-review/crop-app-trustrow.png`.

The height gap has two causes that compound. The app wraps each item in a padded card (16px top and bottom), and its first note is longer: `Gotówką lub kartą w dniu odbioru` wraps to two lines inside the 251px column, where the design's `Gotówka lub karta na miejscu` fits one line in 267px. The columns differ in width because the design's left column is wider overall (1.45fr with 48px page gutters, versus the app's 1.5fr with 32px gutters), which is the §2 grid difference showing up here.

The repeated check icon is the single most visible defect in this row: the design deliberately varies the glyph per promise (payment, insurance, availability), and the app flattens all three to a check.

## Architecture Insights

- Both public date pickers share `src/components/ui/calendar.tsx` (shadcn wrapper over `react-day-picker`). The listing popover uses it with defaults; the detail widget overrides `className`, `classNames.month_caption`, `formatters.formatCaption` and `modifiersClassNames`. Any calendar restyle therefore has two options: change the shared wrapper (affects the listing popover, the staff pickers only if they use it) or pass a fuller `classNames` map from `BookingWidget` only.
- The design's half-cell treatment is a `DayCell` component with a `clipPath` triangle and an SVG divider line; the app's is two CSS utilities with `linear-gradient` fills on the `td`. The staff manual-reservation picker already ships the design's darker `#D7DCE3` + divider (`--flota-busy`, `--flota-busy-divider`), so the tokens exist.
- `VehicleDetail.astro` is an Astro shell with two islands (`VehicleGallery` `client:idle`, `BookingWidget` `client:load`). The hero restructure (title outside the card, separate image card) is pure Astro markup; the widget and calendar changes are React.
- The design uses weight 540 / 650 in-betweens, which the app's variable Inter renders (`font-[650]` is already used on the listing). The detail page uses `font-semibold` (600) and `font-bold` (700) throughout.
- Design tokens map cleanly: `tokens.hair` → `--flota-hair`, `hair2` → `--flota-hair-2`, `accentSoft` → `--flota-accent-soft` / `bg-accent`, `greenSoft` → no utility yet (`bg-success/10` is used instead), `#D7DCE3` → `--flota-busy`, `#A9B2BE` → `--flota-busy-divider`.
