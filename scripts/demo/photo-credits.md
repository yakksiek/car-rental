# Demo photo credits

The demo dataset (`demo.load()`, `supabase/migrations/20260914120000_demo_dataset.sql`) hot-links these
photos from Wikimedia Commons as vehicle catalog images. `scripts/demo/upload-protocol-assets.mjs`
also copies them into the private `protocols` bucket as handover-inspection photos. All are free
licenses. None is modified beyond Wikimedia's own 960 px thumbnail scaling.
